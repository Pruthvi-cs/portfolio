01IDENTITY — WHO'S BUILDINGMITE · COMPUTER SCIENCE

Hardware in one hand,
software in the other.

Most developers pick a side — the cloud or the circuit. I work the seam between them. As a Computer Science student at MITE, I build in both directions: firmware for ESP32 boards in one window, computer-vision pipelines in the next.

The work keeps circling one idea — intelligent systems that are useful in the real world. Cameras that watch for danger. Devices you can command from a chat app. APIs that stand behind safety features. I document what's implemented, test what breaks, and say plainly what's still in progress.
